# ServeChain - Community Service and Volunteering System

A blockchain platform for tracking community service, rewarding volunteer contributions, and managing social impact projects.

## Features
- Volunteer activity tracking and rewards
- Community impact level progression
- Service project management with impact incentives
- Community achievement publishing and validation
- Volunteer coordination and leadership programs

## Smart Contract Functions
- Track volunteer service sessions
- Manage community service projects with impact rewards
- Publish community achievements with peer validation
- Coordinate volunteer programs and initiatives
- Claim community service and impact rewards

## Usage
Promotes community engagement through blockchain-verified service tracking and social impact rewards.
